# Design Constraints Essay
**Project:** JPEG vs. PNG Steganography 
**Team:** Carter Smith, Alex Watts

---

## Professional

Professionally, we are not security experts or cryptographers who specialize in least significant bit (LSB) steganography. However, we do not need to be experts to conduct an interesting and novel experiment for our CS Conference submission. Although greater expertise might strengthen the project's credibility, the proposed experiment remains within our technical capabilities. Our relative lack of expertise may also help us evaluate whether these techniques are accessible to individuals with a general computer science background.

---

## Economic

The project has a $0 budget. All tools, libraries, and datasets must be free or open-source. This rules out paid cloud GPU instances, paid dataset licenses, and proprietary steganalysis tools, and forces the choice of BOSSBase (free via kagglehub), Python scientific libraries (all BSD-licensed), and chi-square as the baseline detector (no compute cost). The tension between Professional and Economic is concrete: a credentialed security research lab would have access to commercial tools and GPU compute that could strengthen the results, but the budget constraint forces every choice to the open-source alternatives that happen to be the standard in academic steganalysis research.

---

## Ethical

At the same time, our findings could potentially help malicious actors conceal information more effectively. Our project has a dual use which raises important ethical questions about how we present and share our results. Ultimately, we believe the project's potential to support beneficial research and improve security practices outweighs the risk of misuse.
